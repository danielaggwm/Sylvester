How to read

Each circle is a cancer type. Bigger circle = more patient samples.
Inner ring = where the cancer starts (body site, e.g. Lung). Rings further out = more specific diagnoses (e.g. Lung Adenocarcinoma).
Outer bars: one bar per specific diagnosis. Longer bar = more samples. It flickers on activity and grows when a new sample arrives.
Orange spark = a new patient sample. It travels from the center to its cancer type; the circle grows and the total goes up.
Green ripple = a file update for an existing sample: received, quality-checked, or loaded.
Spotlight highlights one body site at a time. Its details are on the left.
