How to read

Each circle is a cancer type. Bigger circle = more patient samples.
Inner ring = where the cancer starts (body site, e.g. Lung). Rings further out = more specific diagnoses (e.g. Lung Adenocarcinoma).
Outer bars: one bar per specific diagnosis. Longer bar = more samples. A bar flickers when activity reaches it.
Gold spark = a new patient sample. It travels from the center to its cancer type, then the total goes up.
Blue ripple = a file update for an existing sample: received, quality-checked, or loaded.
Spotlight highlights one body site at a time; its details are on the left.
